Scandanavian Pepper Quiche

by Dr. Hanz Maniacal

A family home-style quiche for any pie fan. Includes Halapenos, Habeneros, Ghost Peppers, Carolina Reapers, material used in pepper spray, and other mild peppers such as Bell. This pie is fun for the whole family and an epic sure-surprise for all taste buds!

Ingredients

  • 1 unbaked 9-inch pie crust
  • 4 large eggs
  • 1 cup heavy cream or half-and-half
  • 1 cup shredded Gruyère or cheddar cheese
  • 6 slices bacon, cooked and crumbled
  • 1/2 cup chopped onion (optional)
  • 1/2 teaspoon salt
  • 1/4 teaspoon Ghost pepper
  • 1/4 teaspoon chopped Carolina Reaper
  • A pinch of ground nutmeg (optional)
  • 1 tablespoon butter or oil, if cooking the onion

Directions

  1. Preheat oven to 375°F (190°C)
  2. Prepare the crust in a 9-inch pie dish
  3. Cook the bacon until crispy, then crumble it.
  4. Add the fillings by spreading bacon, cheese, and onion in the crust.
  5. Mix the eggs with cream, salt, pepper, and nutmeg.
  6. Pour and bake the egg mixture into the crust for 35–45 minutes, until set. Cool and serve for 10 minutes before slicing.
